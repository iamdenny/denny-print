Small ideas for a great afternoon

For a party, fill bingo squares with things guests might have in common. For a nature walk, use things to spot along the way. A maze makes a perfect quiet-time activity; each edition creates a new, solvable route.

For word search, start with a short theme: planets, animals, or favorite foods. Words run left to right, top to bottom, or diagonally downward. Show answers to print a separate answer sheet, then hide them before printing the puzzle.
